## The pitch

> The Blackmail Toothbrush hums your least favourite song on loop until you stop quitting after thirty seconds and brush for the full two minutes.

The tiny everyday annoyance: quitting brushing after thirty seconds.
The ridiculous invention: a toothbrush that blackmails you with your least
favourite song until you brush for the full two minutes.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the one-sentence pitch to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set), after self-checking the
gate: 1–220 characters, exactly one sentence.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
